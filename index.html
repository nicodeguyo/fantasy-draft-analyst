Know what informs the advice

The skill can combine supported projection feeds, rescore their statistics for your league, and incorporate dated news and usage evidence. It shows gaps when the available evidence is incomplete.

What to know before relying on the advice
  • Projections and modeled opponent behavior can be wrong. Utility is a decision score, not a prediction of wins or additional season points.
  • Data refreshes when you request and run an update. Recording a pick updates the draft state, not the underlying player news.
  • Coverage and wait comparisons depend on available data and explicit assumptions. Source disagreement does not automatically disqualify a recommendation.
  • Supported: snake or linear redraft, zero or one keeper per team, and supported scoring and lineup rules. Dynasty, multi-keeper optimization, and auction bidding are outside the shipped draft model.

I built Fantasy Draft Analyst for my own league because rankings alone couldn’t explain the tradeoffs I was making. Drafting with it exposed weaknesses in the original approach.

This release adds multiple-source evidence, actual draft context, and clearer assumptions—so you can inspect the advice and challenge it.
