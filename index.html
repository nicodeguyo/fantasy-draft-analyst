A model you can
check yourself.

Across 800 simulated drafts, the saved-board policy produced 90 more projected starting-lineup points than the noisy ADP-based draft bot, on average.

Metric: sum of projections for one best legal starting lineup. Uses the same saved projections and opponent model. This is not a real-world backtest or a comparison with another draft assistant.

Make your draft plan.

A skill is a package of instructions and scripts you add to Claude. This one guides the player research, runs the simulator, and builds your board. You need Claude with Skills and code execution available on your plan.

  1. Add the skillDownload the ZIP and upload it in Claude’s Skills settings.
  2. Describe your leagueUse the prompt below. Tell Claude your rules, draft slot, and keeper options—or say “no keepers.”
  3. Bring your plan to draft nightReview the sources and assumptions. Download your board, then open it in your browser to track picks.
